Pill Bee - Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 27, 2026

Overview

Pill Bee ("the App") is a pharmacy pill counting application developed by Friendly Pills. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information the App collects and how it is used.

Information We Collect

The App collects minimal information necessary to operate:

  • Apple ID Authentication: When you sign in with Apple, we receive a unique anonymous identifier and, optionally, your email address. This is used solely to authenticate your account and manage your subscription.
  • Subscription Data: Your subscription status is managed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not process or store any payment information.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect analytics or usage data
  • We do not use third-party tracking or advertising services
  • We do not access, store, or transmit camera images or video outside of the device
  • We do not collect personal health information
  • We do not share any data with third parties

Camera Usage

The App uses an external camera to count pills in real time. All camera processing happens entirely on your device using on-device machine learning. No images or video are ever transmitted, stored, or sent to any server.

Data Storage

All app data, including pill count records and user preferences, is stored locally on your device. No data is transmitted to external servers.

Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from within the App by going to Settings and tapping "Delete Account." This will permanently remove all locally stored data and sign you out.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
